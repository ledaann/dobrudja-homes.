Real estate in Dobrich, Balchik and Varna: a home and an investment

Real estate for sale in the Dobrich – Balchik – Varna region: a home to live in and an income-generating property.Direct from the owner, no commission. Quiet, relaxed, the sea close by.

Why the Dobrich region?

Dobrudzha is the calm side of Bulgaria, without crowds of tourists. The sea is only 30–40 km away, yet you can live here comfortably all year round – without scorching heat and with little snow in winter – enjoy thermal springs on the coast, and have all the amenities of civilisation within easy reach. It is a region of well-equipped beaches with full infrastructure, wild coves and long sandy dunes, where summers are quiet and secluded.

You will be charmed by Balchik with the palace of Queen Marie and its Botanical Garden, Kavarna with its spa complexes and golf clubs, Albena with its excellent hotels, pools and guarded grounds, and Varna with its thermal springs, airport, beautiful park and big-city infrastructure. All of this is within about 50 km of the property.

  • Transport. Buses run from Dobrich to Varna roughly every 15 minutes, and on the renovated road the trip takes about 45 minutes. There is also a rail connection. Varna international airport served 60 destinations in the summer 2026 season, including Paris, Berlin, Munich, Vienna, Rome, Barcelona, Warsaw and Istanbul.
  • Fertile land. Dobrudzha is traditionally called the breadbasket of Bulgaria. A plot here is a chance to have a garden, a vegetable patch and a small farm of your own.
  • Well-developed infrastructure. Towns and resort areas have shops, schools, hospitals, transport and services. Villages near Dobrich have kindergartens and schools, good transport links and decent roads.
  • Investment appeal. Near Varna and Balchik, property prices remain lower than in the popular coastal resorts, and demand is supported by the sea, the airport and growing interest in living not only right on the coast.
  • Labour market. Unemployment in the region is falling: in 2025 it was 5.8%, 0.7 percentage points lower than a year earlier, which makes the region more stable.
